Privacy Policy for FrostyFrisby games

Last updated: 6 October 2026

This policy covers Tidy Bot and Tidy Bot Demo for Windows, and Tidy Bot: Robot Vacuum Game for Android, published by FrostyFrisby.

What we collect

Nothing. FrostyFrisby does not collect, store or receive any personal information. The games have no accounts, no sign-in and no analytics of our own.

Ads in the Android version

The Android game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google and its advertising partners may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address (which shows your approximate location), how you interact with ads, and diagnostic information. This is handled under Google's Privacy Policy.

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland the game asks for your consent before showing ads, and you can change your choice at any time under Privacy options in the pause menu. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. The Windows versions contain no ads.

What stays on your device

Your game progress and settings (unlocked rooms, coins, upgrades, sound options) are saved on your own device only. Uninstalling the game removes them.

Third parties

Apart from the advertising described above, the games share no data with anyone. The Microsoft Store and Google Play handle your downloads and purchases under their own privacy statements.

Children

The Windows games are suitable for all ages and collect no information from anyone, including children. The Android game is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
