Privacy Policy for FrostyFrisby games

Last updated: 5 October 2026

This policy covers Tidy Bot and Tidy Bot Demo for Windows, published by FrostyFrisby.

What we collect

Nothing. The games do not collect, store or send any personal information. They have no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no online features.

What stays on your device

Your game progress and settings (unlocked rooms, coins, upgrades, sound options) are saved on your own computer only. They are never sent to us or anyone else. Uninstalling the game removes them.

Third parties

The games do not share data with third parties. If you get the game from the Microsoft Store, Microsoft handles your purchase and download under its own privacy statement.

Children

The games are suitable for all ages and collect no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact and support

Questions or problems with a game? Email wesley@frostyfrisby.com.
